# Q1 worksheet — 17 pages that already exist

These URLs are **live today** and already carry 387 of your high-priority keywords. No new pages are needed for any of them.

This is a metadata and heading rewrite — the fastest ranking work available to you, and the right place to start.

For each page: set the title tag and meta description, make the H1 match the primary keyword, and work the secondary keywords into H2s and body copy naturally.

**Excluded as primaries on purpose:** misspellings (they belong in body copy or schema `alternateName`), "near me" terms (won through your Google Business Profile, not a page title), and superlatives like "best" (you cannot assert these about yourself — they are earned through reviews). All three still appear as secondaries where they are legitimate targets.
